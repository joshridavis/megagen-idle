# Press kit

**MegaGen Idle** is an idle game about generating energy. Build an energy empire, from one solar panel to a micro-supernova. It keeps generating while you're away.

## Fact sheet

- **Developer and publisher:** Joshri Games, a solo developer in Israel
- **Release date:** March 11, 2027
- **Platforms:** Windows (Steam, plays on Steam Deck), Android, iPhone and iPad; the free part in any browser at megagenidle.com
- **Price:** free to start; the Full Game is a one-time purchase (US$6.99 on Steam, US$4.99 on Android and iPhone)
- **Business model:** buy once. No ads, no premium currency, never pay-to-win. The Supporter Pack (US$2.99) is cosmetic only.
- **Languages:** English at launch
- **Website:** https://megagenidle.com/

## About the game

Start with one clumsy solar panel. Dig coal, pump oil, research smarter machines and fill your site with wind farms, dams and reactors, all the way to a micro supernova in a fast-time dimension. Your machines keep making energy while you are away.
