# Privacy

This page says what MegaGen Idle stores about you and how to remove it. The full privacy policy follows before the launch.

## Playing without an account

Your game is saved only on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

## With an account

When you create an account, we store:

- your email address, to sign in and to reset your password;
- the username you choose, which may be shown on leaderboards in the future;
- your cloud saves (game progress only) and when they were made;
- when the account was created.

We do not store your real name, payment details or anything about other websites you visit, and we do not sell or share your data. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are stored only as secure hashes; we never see them.

## Where it is kept

Accounts and cloud saves are kept by Supabase, our database provider, in the region chosen for the game.

## Purchases

Purchases are made through Steam, Google Play or the App Store. They handle the payment; we only learn which items your copy owns.

## This website

The website sets no cookies and runs no tracking code. Our host counts page visits without cookies and without identifying you.

## Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings, then Account and cloud saves, and choose "Delete my account". Your login, username and every cloud save are deleted right away. The game saved on your device stays until you reset it in Settings.
