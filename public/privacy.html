MegaGen Idle: Privacy

This page says what the game stores about you and how to remove it.

Playing without an account

Your game is saved only in your browser on this device. Nothing is sent to us.

With an account

When you create an account, we store:

  • your email address, to sign in and to reset your password;
  • the username you choose, which may be shown on leaderboards in the future;
  • your cloud saves (game progress only) and when they were made;
  • when the account was created.

We do not store your real name, payment details or anything about other websites you visit, and we do not sell or share your data. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are stored only as secure hashes; we never see them.

Where it is kept

Accounts and cloud saves are kept by Supabase, our database provider, in the region chosen for the game.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings, then Account and cloud saves, and choose "Delete my account". Your login, username and every cloud save are deleted right away. The game saved in your browser stays until you reset it in Settings.

Questions

Open an issue on the game's GitHub page.
