# Balance report

Generated by `npm run simulate` for v0.29.0 in 49097 ms. Deterministic: the same data always gives the same report.

**Player model:** greedy and idle-heavy. Clicks 2/s for the first 10 minutes, then never again. Decides every 60 s for the first 30 h, then every 300 s:
1. Start the affordable research that unlocks something (or else the quickest one).
2. Build the unlocked generator with the most energy per room that it can afford and fuel, scrapping weaker generators when room is short.
3. Buy a producer for whatever resource blocks its next build.
4. Expand room when it is nearly full.

A real player who clicks more or plans better will be faster.

## Tuning log

### v0.28.x (item 1.54, eight more decorations)

- **Decorations:** 14 kinds (was 6). New first-copy prices: Flower bed 2K (level 8), Bench 10K (10 achievements), Hedge 30K (level 12), Rock garden 200K (25 contracts), Picnic table 500K (level 30), Fountain 3M (25 achievements), Weather station 5M (level 45), Memorial plaque 20M (100 contracts); x1.6 per copy, 6 of each for 100%.
- **Simulator:** each decoration copy bought counts as progress (the Completion tab shows n/6 bought), like every 5 contracts; all 6 of a kind is listed as a milestone.
- **Stall threshold:** `STALL_HOURS` went from 12 to 13 (owner decision, 2026-10-10). The late game is close to 12 h during the about 60-hour Fast-Time Dimension research, where only contracts mark progress (the closest gap before was 11.6 h). With the new decorations the contract timing shifted and one gap became 12.7 h (427.7 h to 440.4 h); thirteen price and simulator variants all left a gap of 12 h or more somewhere between 330 h and 500 h.
- **Result:** 100% completion at 761.4 h (was 761.3 h). No stalls at 13 h.

### v0.27.x (item 1.88, three more room expansions)

- **Owner, playtest 26:** every room expansion was bought within two weeks. Tiers 11 to 13 (`src/data/rooms.ts`): +300, +380 and +480 room; 1.8B, 3.6B and 4.8B energy (written 150M, 300M, 400M, times the late-room factors), metal 160k to 260k, stone, uranium 200 to 400 and deuterium 60 to 150; player levels 80, 88 and 95. The item suggested levels 100 and 120, but the player level stops at 99. The simulator reaches level 99 at about 325 h, before room expansion 10, so energy and metal are what hold these tiers back, not the level.
- **Stall threshold:** `STALL_HOURS` went from 8 to 12, as 1.87 allowed: while saving for the late expansions the simulated player buys little else, and waits of 9 to 12 hours remained after level 99. The first costs tried (up to 1.2B written energy for tier 13, 2.4x more) made waits of up to 17 h, so the costs were lowered until no wait passed 12 h.
- **Result:** 100% completion at 761.3 h (was 825.9 h after 1.87: the extra room speeds up the very end). Key times: 75% 306.9 h, room expansion 10 at 326.5 h, 11 at 383.1 h, 12 at 456.5 h, 13 at 496.4 h, first Micro-Supernova 440.4 h. No stalls.

- **Effect: 100% completion moved from about 81.5 h to about 66.5 h.** The bonus compounds through the long upgrade stretch at the end. Early milestones barely move. This works against the 200 h target; the coming content items (effect events, contracts, pets, fictional generators) and the 0.47 pass make up for it.

### v0.11.0 (item 0.88, player level)

- **Player level** comes from lifetime energy: level L needs 0.05 × (L − 1)^5.3 energy, up to a maximum of 99. In the simulator, level 10 arrives at about 1 h, level 50 at about 30 h and level 76 at 100% completion (81.5 h). With today's energy rates, level 99 would take about 190 h; retune in 0.47 as content raises output.

### v0.11.0 (item 0.83, click-power research)

- **Added** five click research: Ergonomic Handle (+100%), Flywheel (+200%) and Geared Crank (+400% energy per click); then Kinetic Capture and Grid Tap, each adding a quarter second of current energy/s to every click (capped at half a second).
- **No pacing change:** the simulated player clicks only in its first 10 minutes. 100% completion is at about 81.5 h, with no stalls.

### v0.10.0 (item 0.33, tier 3: oil and nuclear)

- **Added:** Oil Power Plant (20 energy/s, 10 room, 6 oil/h) and Nuclear Fission Plant (60 energy/s, 12 room, 1 uranium/h). Each gives 2.5 times the energy per room of the tier before. New resources oil and uranium; producers Oil Rig (1 oil per 5 min) and Uranium Mine (1 uranium per 30 min); the first of each is granted by research. There are 7 new research: Oil Drilling, Oil Refining, Uranium Mining, Nuclear Fission, Reinforced Concrete, Heat Recovery and Reactor Safety Systems.

- **100% completion: about 82 h** (was about 52 h). Most of the late game is raising Oil and Nuclear plants to level 10.

### v0.10.0 (item 0.82, max upgrades count toward 100%)

- **Completion now includes every generator type at max level** (owner, playtest 9). Built types and best levels are permanent records, so scrapping never lowers completion.
- **Simulator player:** once research and room are done, it raises each generator type to max level, building one of any type it lacks (scrapping spare copies for room). Each new best level counts as a milestone for stall detection; the table above lists only the max-level ones.
- **Result:** 100% completion at about **52 h** (was about 25 h). No stalls. The last stretch, 25–52 h, is raising Gas, Tidal and Hydro from level 7 to 10. Still short of the 200 h target; tier 3 (0.33) and later content add more.

### v0.9.0 (item 0.32, generator upgrades)

- **Upgrades added:** +25% base output per level, max level 10. Energy cost is the build energy × 1.6 per level.
- **First simulated run with upgrades:** 100% completion took 148 h with three stalls of 30–50 h each, waiting on room tiers 6–8.
- **Bug found and fixed (game):** the Gas Well granted by Natural Gas Extraction took room even when the room was full, putting it over capacity (65/63). Producers granted by research now take no room.
- **Tuning (data):**
  - Upgrade resource costs now grow ×1.3 per level instead of ×1.6, so upgrades are mainly an energy sink. Before, level-8 upgrades needed about 40K metal and starved the room tiers.
  - Room tiers 6–8 cost less metal, stone and coal: tier 6 3K/1.5K/150, tier 7 5K/2.5K/300, tier 8 10K/5K/600 + 50 gas. Energy costs are unchanged.
- **Simulator player:** saves energy and resources for the next room tier when room is nearly full, and buys producers for what that tier lacks.
- **Result:** no stalls; all near-term targets met. 100% completion at about 25 h, still content-limited (see below).
- **Open question for the owner:** should "100% completion" include every generator type at max level? With upgrades that would add many hours, and it is a natural "perfection" goal.

### v0.9.0 (item 0.35, first run)

- **No data changes.** All near-term targets are met: first generator at once, first research about 12 min, Wind about 4 h, Coal about 2.6 h, Hydro about 3.6 h, Gas plants about 19 h. There are no stalls.
- **100% completion takes about 25 h, far short of the owner's 200 h target.** This is deliberate, not tuned yet. The owner wants to judge total playtime after more content exists (playtest 8), and the game today has only 6 generator types, 23 research and 8 room tiers. Stretching today's numbers 8× would make the early game drag.
- **Planned route to about 200 h:** each content item (0.32 upgrades, 0.33 tier 3, 0.34 fictional, 0.44 more methods, 0.45 deeper tree, 0.65 achievements) adds hours. Item 0.47 then tunes the whole curve against this report.
