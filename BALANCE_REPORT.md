# Balance report

Generated by `npm run simulate` for v0.26.0 in 50499 ms. Deterministic: the same data always gives the same report.

**Player model:** greedy and idle-heavy. Clicks 2/s for the first 10 minutes, then never again. Decides every 60 s:
1. Start the affordable research that unlocks something (or else the quickest one).
2. Build the unlocked generator with the most energy per room that it can afford and fuel, scrapping weaker generators when room is short.
3. Buy a producer for whatever resource blocks its next build.
4. Expand room when it is nearly full.

A real player who clicks more or plans better will be faster.

## Tuning log

### v0.26.x (item 1.86, fuel producers retuned to the burners they feed)

- **Owner, playtest 26:** one Deuterium Extractor with 4 Fusion Reactors still gained 13.7 deuterium per hour; oil and natural gas piled up the same way. The old ratios were set on base numbers, but late-game boosts raise producer output (research +45%, zone +10 to +20%, Robot Dog +10%, a resource pet up to +30%) and cut fuel burn (research −45%).
- **New rates** (`src/data/producers.ts`), checked by `burnersFedByOne` in `src/utils/fuelBalance.ts` with every late boost on both sides: Gas Well 1 per 35 min (was 10 min): feeds 3.0 Natural Gas Plants (was 10.4). Oil Rig 1 per 16 min (was 5 min): 2.0 Oil Power Plants (was 6.4). Uranium Mine 1 per 100 min (was 30 min): 2.2 Fission Plants (was 7.5). Deuterium Extractor 1 per 90 min (was 6 min): 2.0 Fusion Reactors or 1.0 Micro-Supernova (was 30 reactors).
- **Coal Mine unchanged:** it feeds 3 Coal Plants at the start, as designed, and about 10 with every late boost. It is the first fuel, a 1-room producer, and coal also pays for builds and pet food; slowing it would slow the first hours, which 1.87 must keep as they are.
- **Simulator player:** its fuel planning already buys a producer whenever a fuel is burned faster than made (with a 50% surplus), so it buys more wells, rigs, mines and extractors and they take room. No change was needed.
- **Result:** 100% completion at 327.3 h (was 293.3 h). Key times: 50% 42.1 h (was 37.4 h), first Fusion Reactor 97.7 h (was 93.1 h), 75% 97.8 h (was 93.1 h), room expansion 10 at 113.7 h (was 97.0 h), first Micro-Supernova 186.7 h. No stalls.
