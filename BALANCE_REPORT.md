# Balance report

Generated by `npm run simulate` for v0.23.0 in 23204 ms. Deterministic: the same data always gives the same report.

**Player model:** greedy and idle-heavy. Clicks 2/s for the first 10 minutes, then never again. Decides every 60 s:
1. Start the affordable research that unlocks something (or else the quickest one).
2. Build the unlocked generator with the most energy per room that it can afford and fuel, scrapping weaker generators when room is short.
3. Buy a producer for whatever resource blocks its next build.
4. Expand room when it is nearly full.

A real player who clicks more or plans better will be faster.

## Tuning log

### v0.24.0 (item 1.59, up to 3 active pets)

- **Pet slots:** the 2nd active slot costs 100M energy and needs player level 45; the 3rd costs 1B and needs level 80. Bonuses of different active pets add up.
- **Simulator player:** buys a slot with 1.5 times its price in hand above room savings, and keeps the Static Cat, the Wind-up Robot Dog and the Glowing Jellyfish active (the widest bonuses). Slot 2 at 88.0 h, slot 3 at 243.6 h.
- **Tried:** 50M and 600M made 100% at 234.7 h (the stacked bonuses speed up the end game); 100M and 2B never bought the 3rd slot within 400 h.
- **Result:** 100% completion at 254.8 h (was 267.6 h after 1.57). No stalls.

### v0.24.0 (item 1.57, one pet grows at a time; bonus 1×, 2×, 4× by stage)

- **Pets:** only one pet grows at a time. Each pet's bonus is now Baby 1×, Young 2×, Adult 4× of a base value; the adult bonus is unchanged (for example the Static Cat: +0.75%, +1.5%, +3%, was +1%, +2%, +3%).
- **Simulator player:** feeds whichever pet it can, one at a time. Pets fully grown: Electric Eel 28.5 h, Magnetic Beetle 38.5 h, Wheel Hamster 46.5 h, Solar Tortoise 56.5 h, Firefly Swarm 66.5 h, Static Cat 78.5 h, Glowing Jellyfish 86.5 h, Wind-up Robot Dog 122.8 h.
- **Result:** 100% completion at 267.6 h (was 250.1 h after 1.53). No stalls.

### v0.24.0 (item 1.53, decorations bought with energy)

- **Prices:** each decoration is bought with energy when placed; the first copy costs Tree 1K, Company flag 5K, Pond 100K, Windsock 250K, Lamp post 1M, Founder statue 10M, and each next copy of a kind ×1.6. Removing one refunds nothing.
- **Completion:** a new "Decorations" part: every kind bought once, plus 25 decorations bought in all (7 entries). New achievements First decoration, 10 decorations and Every kind count too; 50 decorations is a bonus achievement.
- **Simulator player:** buys the cheapest decoration completion still needs when it has 4 times its price in hand above room savings, on a free tile. First decoration 2.2 h, 10 at 21.7 h, every kind 91.4 h (the statue waits for 50 contracts and 40M in hand).
- **Result:** 100% completion at 250.1 h (was 264.7 h). The small early purchases shift the greedy player's early order (first Hydropower Dam 4.6 h, was 5.5 h; room expansion 8 at 21.6 h, was 28.6 h), which ends up a little faster; still above the 200 h target. No stalls.

### v0.23.0 (items 1.62 and 1.61, harder research and player level gates)

- **Owner, playtest 22:**
  - research level requirements were too low (the top research needed level 14; the owner was at 21 within a week);
  - the player level should be a hard requirement for room expansions and contract perks.
- **Research (1.62):** levels 1-6 stay. Upper requirements are stretched in order: 7→9, 8→13, 9→17, 10→21, 11→26, 12→31, 13→35, 14→38. Stellar Harvest now needs all but one of the other research done. No change in the simulation: the simulated player already researches in tree order.
- **Player level (1.61):**
  - Room expansions need player level 1, 3, 5, 8, 10, 13, 17, 22, 50 and 70, a little above where the simulator reached each one.
  - Contract perk levels need player level 10, 30, 55 and 75 (1st to 4th level of a perk).
- **Result:** **100% at about 264.7 h** (was 251.0 h). No stalls.
  - Expansion 4 at 5.2 h (was 4.9 h), 6 at 10.9 h (was 9.1 h), 8 at 28.6 h (was 19.9 h), 10 at 92.1 h (was 87.2 h).
  - First Fusion Reactor 120.5 h (was 113.5 h); every contract perk 176.3 h (was 173.1 h).

### v0.19.4 (item 1.32, costlier materials, harder room, longer research)

- **Owner, playtest 19.3:**
  - metal and stone costs too low everywhere;
  - room expansions slightly harder;
  - research slightly longer.
- **Changes** (`src/data/balance.ts`):
  - every metal and stone cost ×2 (builds, upgrades, producers, research, room, pet food, and the starting stock);
  - room expansions +20% energy;
  - research +25% time, rounded to whole minutes.
- **Result:** **100% at about 259 h** (was 211 h). No stalls.
  - Early milestones barely move (Wind 2.3 h, Coal 3.2 h, Hydro 5.5 h); the slowdown is in the mid and late game.
  - First Nuclear 55 h (was 44 h), first Fusion 118 h (was 79 h), every contract perk 174 h, first Micro-Supernova 217 h (was 176 h).
- **Simulator speed:** about 30 s per run (would have been about 45 s). Research bonuses are cached per list, and the player skips scrap-and-build attempts it cannot pay for or that found no spot before.

### v0.13.0 (item 0.86, Grid Contracts)

- **Contracts:** unlock at research level 3, with 3 slots and a new offer every 30 minutes. They are sized from current output: deliver energy (20–60 min of energy/s), deliver two resources (30–90 min of production), or produce energy (30–90 min). Rewards: materials (45 min of metal, stone and coal), +25% energy for 30 minutes, or 1–3 Contract Points. There are 4 perks (5 levels) for 43 points in total.
- **Completion:** gains milestones at 10, 50, 100 and 200 contracts, plus every perk level. A first try with 10/25/50/100 finished at 65 h: contract rewards speed everything up, and 100 contracts arrive at about 61 h, before the rest is done.
- **Simulator player:** claims points until every perk is bought, then materials. It delivers whenever doing so keeps what it is saving for the next room tier.
- **Result:** every perk at about 25 h. **100% at about 102 h** (was 67.7 h); the last stretch is reaching 200 contracts. No stalls.

### v0.13.0 (item 0.85, random events with effects)

- **Added 13 effect events:** 7 positive and 6 negative. They roll at half the rate of sightings, and negative ones at 75% of that (owner: slightly rarer). On average that is about one effect event every 2 hours of eligible time.
- **Simulator:** the greedy player plays without events. Their average effect is small and roughly balanced: the timed boosts and penalties are similar in size, and grants are a few minutes of output. Leaving them out keeps the report deterministic.
- **Result:** 100% in about 67.7 h (unchanged).

### v0.13.0 (item 0.95, slower early player levels)

- **Curve:** level L now needs 200 × (L − 1)^3.56 lifetime energy (was 0.05 × (L − 1)^5.3). Level 2 needs 200 energy, about 100 s of fast clicking, instead of a single click.
- **Levels:**
  - Level 6 at about 3 h, 16 at about 12 h, 31 at about 30 h, and 53 at 100%.
  - Level 99 needs about 2.5 billion lifetime energy, near the 200 h target once content grows.
- **Result:** the smaller bonus at each point in time moves 100% from about 66.5 h to about 67.7 h. No stalls.

### v0.9.0 (item 0.32, generator upgrades)

- **Upgrades added:** +25% base output per level, max level 10. Energy cost is the build energy × 1.6 per level.
- **First simulated run with upgrades:** 100% completion took 148 h with three stalls of 30–50 h each, waiting on room tiers 6–8.
- **Bug found and fixed (game):** the Gas Well granted by Natural Gas Extraction took room even when the room was full, putting it over capacity (65/63). Producers granted by research now take no room.
- **Tuning (data):**
  - Upgrade resource costs now grow ×1.3 per level instead of ×1.6, so upgrades are mainly an energy sink. Before, level-8 upgrades needed about 40K metal and starved the room tiers.
  - Room tiers 6–8 cost less metal, stone and coal: tier 6 3K/1.5K/150, tier 7 5K/2.5K/300, tier 8 10K/5K/600 + 50 gas. Energy costs are unchanged.
- **Simulator player:** saves energy and resources for the next room tier when room is nearly full, and buys producers for what that tier lacks.
- **Result:** no stalls; all near-term targets met. 100% completion at about 25 h, still content-limited (see below).
- **Open question for the owner:** should "100% completion" include every generator type at max level? With upgrades that would add many hours, and it is a natural "perfection" goal.
