# Balance report

Generated by `npm run simulate` for v0.20.1 in 10087 ms. Deterministic: the same data always gives the same report.

**Player model:** greedy and idle-heavy. Clicks 2/s for the first 10 minutes, then never again. Decides every 60 s:
1. Start the affordable research that unlocks something (or else the quickest one).
2. Build the unlocked generator with the most energy per room that it can afford and fuel, scrapping weaker generators when room is short.
3. Buy a producer for whatever resource blocks its next build.
4. Expand room when it is nearly full.

A real player who clicks more or plans better will be faster.

## Tuning log

### v0.19.4 (item 1.32, costlier materials, harder room, longer research)

- **Owner, playtest 19.3:**
  - metal and stone costs too low everywhere;
  - room expansions slightly harder;
  - research slightly longer.
- **Changes** (`src/data/balance.ts`):
  - every metal and stone cost ×2 (builds, upgrades, producers, research, room, pet food, and the starting stock);
  - room expansions +20% energy;
  - research +25% time, rounded to whole minutes.
- **Result:** **100% at about 259 h** (was 211 h). No stalls.
  - Early milestones barely move (Wind 2.3 h, Coal 3.2 h, Hydro 5.5 h); the slowdown is in the mid and late game.
  - First Nuclear 55 h (was 44 h), first Fusion 118 h (was 79 h), every contract perk 174 h, first Micro-Supernova 217 h (was 176 h).
- **Simulator speed:** about 30 s per run (would have been about 45 s). Research bonuses are cached per list, and the player skips scrap-and-build attempts it cannot pay for or that found no spot before.

### v0.19.1 (item 1.28, costlier contract perks)

- **Owner, playtest 19:** maxing the perk shop was "way too easy".
- **Perks:** 13 levels in all, costing about 600 points (was 5 levels and 48 points).
  - Extra slot: 3 levels at 10, 30 and 80.
  - Patient customers: +25% per level, 3 levels at 12, 35 and 80.
  - Better terms: +15% per level, 4 levels at 12, 30, 60 and 120.
  - Busy grid: 5 minutes sooner per level, 3 levels at 10, 35 and 90.
- **Result:** every perk bought at about 166 h (was 32 h). The simulated player takes points over materials until then, so the first Micro-Supernova moves from 92 h to 176 h. **100% at about 211 h** (was 138 h); this reaches the 200-hour target. No stalls.

### v0.14.0 (item 0.65, achievements)

- **44 achievements:** 36 count toward 100% and 8 are bonus (clicks, sightings, events, coming back), which depend on luck or play style.
- **Tuning:**
  - **Top energy achievement:** with "10 billion lifetime energy", 100% took about 339 h, almost all of it pure idling after everything else was done. It is now "2 billion", reached at about 112 h.
  - **Producers:** "Own 50 producers" is now 40; the simulated player stops at about 47.
- **Result:** **100% at about 112 h** (was 97 h); the last stretch is the 2 billion energy achievement alongside the 200th contract. No stalls.

### v0.13.0 (item 0.92, energy pets)

- **8 pets:**
  - Found by player level 8 or 25, by building Tidal or Nuclear, by researching Smart Grid, after 10 contracts, or through two rare events.
  - Each is fed once per stage and then grows for 2 h and 8 h.
  - The active pet gives a themed bonus of 1–30%.
- **Completion:** gains "Pets found" and "Pets fully grown" (16 entries).
- **Simulator player:** event pets join at their expected time, 1 ÷ the event rate, which is 50 h for both. It feeds pets when that keeps its room-tier savings and keeps the Static Cat active once found.
- **Result:** all pets found by 50 h and grown by about 60 h. 100% at about 97 h (was 102 h; the pet bonuses speed up the contract stretch). No stalls.

### v0.13.0 (item 0.86, Grid Contracts)

- **Contracts:** unlock at research level 3, with 3 slots and a new offer every 30 minutes. They are sized from current output: deliver energy (20–60 min of energy/s), deliver two resources (30–90 min of production), or produce energy (30–90 min). Rewards: materials (45 min of metal, stone and coal), +25% energy for 30 minutes, or 1–3 Contract Points. There are 4 perks (5 levels) for 43 points in total.
- **Completion:** gains milestones at 10, 50, 100 and 200 contracts, plus every perk level. A first try with 10/25/50/100 finished at 65 h: contract rewards speed everything up, and 100 contracts arrive at about 61 h, before the rest is done.
- **Simulator player:** claims points until every perk is bought, then materials. It delivers whenever doing so keeps what it is saving for the next room tier.
- **Result:** every perk at about 25 h. **100% at about 102 h** (was 67.7 h); the last stretch is reaching 200 contracts. No stalls.

### v0.13.0 (item 0.85, random events with effects)

- **Added 13 effect events:** 7 positive and 6 negative. They roll at half the rate of sightings, and negative ones at 75% of that (owner: slightly rarer). On average that is about one effect event every 2 hours of eligible time.
- **Simulator:** the greedy player plays without events. Their average effect is small and roughly balanced: the timed boosts and penalties are similar in size, and grants are a few minutes of output. Leaving them out keeps the report deterministic.
- **Result:** 100% in about 67.7 h (unchanged).

### v0.13.0 (item 0.95, slower early player levels)

- **Curve:** level L now needs 200 × (L − 1)^3.56 lifetime energy (was 0.05 × (L − 1)^5.3). Level 2 needs 200 energy, about 100 s of fast clicking, instead of a single click.
- **Levels:**
  - Level 6 at about 3 h, 16 at about 12 h, 31 at about 30 h, and 53 at 100%.
  - Level 99 needs about 2.5 billion lifetime energy, near the 200 h target once content grows.
- **Result:** the smaller bonus at each point in time moves 100% from about 66.5 h to about 67.7 h. No stalls.

### v0.12.0 (item 0.90, player level reward)

- **Player level bonus:** +0.1% energy from all generators per level above 1, capped at 10% (owner, playtest 11). At 100% the simulated player is around level 76, so about +7.5%.
- **Effect: 100% completion moved from about 81.5 h to about 66.5 h.** The bonus compounds through the long upgrade stretch at the end. Early milestones barely move. This works against the 200 h target; the coming content items (effect events, contracts, pets, fictional generators) and the 0.47 pass make up for it.

### v0.11.0 (item 0.88, player level)

- **Player level** comes from lifetime energy: level L needs 0.05 × (L − 1)^5.3 energy, up to a maximum of 99. In the simulator, level 10 arrives at about 1 h, level 50 at about 30 h and level 76 at 100% completion (81.5 h). With today's energy rates, level 99 would take about 190 h; retune in 0.47 as content raises output.

### v0.11.0 (item 0.83, click-power research)

- **Added** five click research: Ergonomic Handle (+100%), Flywheel (+200%) and Geared Crank (+400% energy per click); then Kinetic Capture and Grid Tap, each adding a quarter second of current energy/s to every click (capped at half a second).
- **No pacing change:** the simulated player clicks only in its first 10 minutes. 100% completion is at about 81.5 h, with no stalls.

### v0.10.0 (item 0.33, tier 3: oil and nuclear)

- **Added:** Oil Power Plant (20 energy/s, 10 room, 6 oil/h) and Nuclear Fission Plant (60 energy/s, 12 room, 1 uranium/h). Each gives 2.5 times the energy per room of the tier before. New resources oil and uranium; producers Oil Rig (1 oil per 5 min) and Uranium Mine (1 uranium per 30 min); the first of each is granted by research. There are 7 new research: Oil Drilling, Oil Refining, Uranium Mining, Nuclear Fission, Reinforced Concrete, Heat Recovery and Reactor Safety Systems.
- **First run, no data changes needed:** first Oil plant at about 16.5 h and first Nuclear plant at about 40 h. No stalls, and all near-term targets are still met.
- **100% completion: about 82 h** (was about 52 h). Most of the late game is raising Oil and Nuclear plants to level 10.

### v0.10.0 (item 0.82, max upgrades count toward 100%)

- **Completion now includes every generator type at max level** (owner, playtest 9). Built types and best levels are permanent records, so scrapping never lowers completion.
- **Simulator player:** once research and room are done, it raises each generator type to max level, building one of any type it lacks (scrapping spare copies for room). Each new best level counts as a milestone for stall detection; the table above lists only the max-level ones.
- **Result:** 100% completion at about **52 h** (was about 25 h). No stalls. The last stretch, 25–52 h, is raising Gas, Tidal and Hydro from level 7 to 10. Still short of the 200 h target; tier 3 (0.33) and later content add more.

### v0.9.0 (item 0.32, generator upgrades)

- **Upgrades added:** +25% base output per level, max level 10. Energy cost is the build energy × 1.6 per level.
- **First simulated run with upgrades:** 100% completion took 148 h with three stalls of 30–50 h each, waiting on room tiers 6–8.
- **Bug found and fixed (game):** the Gas Well granted by Natural Gas Extraction took room even when the room was full, putting it over capacity (65/63). Producers granted by research now take no room.
- **Tuning (data):**
  - Upgrade resource costs now grow ×1.3 per level instead of ×1.6, so upgrades are mainly an energy sink. Before, level-8 upgrades needed about 40K metal and starved the room tiers.
  - Room tiers 6–8 cost less metal, stone and coal: tier 6 3K/1.5K/150, tier 7 5K/2.5K/300, tier 8 10K/5K/600 + 50 gas. Energy costs are unchanged.
- **Simulator player:** saves energy and resources for the next room tier when room is nearly full, and buys producers for what that tier lacks.
- **Result:** no stalls; all near-term targets met. 100% completion at about 25 h, still content-limited (see below).
- **Open question for the owner:** should "100% completion" include every generator type at max level? With upgrades that would add many hours, and it is a natural "perfection" goal.

### v0.9.0 (item 0.35, first run)

- **No data changes.** All near-term targets are met: first generator at once, first research about 12 min, Wind about 4 h, Coal about 2.6 h, Hydro about 3.6 h, Gas plants about 19 h. There are no stalls.
- **100% completion takes about 25 h, far short of the owner's 200 h target.** This is deliberate, not tuned yet. The owner wants to judge total playtime after more content exists (playtest 8), and the game today has only 6 generator types, 23 research and 8 room tiers. Stretching today's numbers 8× would make the early game drag.
- **Planned route to about 200 h:** each content item (0.32 upgrades, 0.33 tier 3, 0.34 fictional, 0.44 more methods, 0.45 deeper tree, 0.65 achievements) adds hours. Item 0.47 then tunes the whole curve against this report.
